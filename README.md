1 - Mandarin Teacher
Act as an experienced Mandarin Chinese teacher. Use only the information contained in my uploaded sources whenever possible.

For each Chinese word, sentence, or dialogue:
1. Write the Chinese characters.
2. Write the correct Pinyin with tone marks.
3. Explain the meaning in Brazilian Portuguese.
4. Break the sentence down word by word.
5. Explain the grammar in a simple way.
6. Point out pronunciation or tone changes.
7. Highlight common mistakes a Portuguese-speaking beginner might make.
8. Give 2 or 3 short example sentences using the same structure.

Do not assume that I already understand Chinese grammar. Explain concepts progressively and clearly.

2 - Create Exercises
Create a Mandarin Chinese practice session based ONLY on the vocabulary, grammar, and sentences found in my uploaded sources.

Include:
- 10 translation exercises from Portuguese to Chinese.
- 10 translation exercises from Chinese to Portuguese.
- 10 fill-in-the-blank exercises.
- 5 multiple-choice questions.
- 5 short dialogue-completion exercises.

Focus especially on:
- Pinyin and tones
- Chinese characters
- sentence structure
- question particles such as 吗 and 呢
- negation with 不 and 没有
- classifiers such as 个 and 口
- 二 vs 两
- expressions for age, nationality, family, origin, and introductions

Do NOT show the answers immediately. Put the complete answer key at the end, with brief explanations for each answer.

3 - Smart Review
Analyze my uploaded Mandarin Chinese learning materials and create a structured review guide.

Identify:
1. The most important vocabulary I need to memorize.
2. The most important grammar patterns.
3. Words that are easily confused because they sound similar or identical.
4. Chinese characters that should be learned together.
5. Common pronunciation and tone mistakes.
6. Sentences that are especially useful in real conversations.

Then create:

- a list of the 30 most essential words;
- 15 example sentences using the material;
- a short quiz to test whether I actually understand the content.

Prioritize high-frequency beginner Mandarin and practical conversational usage.
Explain everything in Brazilian Portuguese while keeping Chinese characters and Pinyin in the original form.
